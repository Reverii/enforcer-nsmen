Day markers for Enforcer NSMEN

Three ways to replace the P, S and R letters. Each option shows the same week on an NSman's calendar and on the planner's grid. Mon 14 Dec is today; Tue 15 and Thu 17 are Loadmaster flight days.

Option B: marking a cancelled or withdrawn day

Grey "Off" reads like a day off. These three say what happened to the SAF100. Each shows the NSman's day (big) and the planner's grid cell (small) for a cancel and a withdraw.

1. Name it, in red

19Cancelled
22Withdrawn
CxlW/d

Says exactly what happened and who did it. Red makes the planner look. The grid needs a short form.

2. SAF100, struck through

19SAF100
22SAF100
SAFSAF

Reads as "this SAF100 is off" with no new word to learn, and the same mark fits the grid. Who cancelled it shows when you tap the day.

3. Back to a plain day, with a red flag

19
22

The day looks free again (the SAF100 day is given back), with a small red corner so the planner knows something changed. Quietest, but the reason is only in the pop-up.
